Android bug: IR Photo Type changed elsewhere does not show after Refresh

Jira: ZP-5200, with the video and four screenshots attached.

  1. On Android, open work order QA-DEMO ZP-5135 FLIR-SEP iOS 1.74 2 Nov › Details. Note the IR Photo Type (FLIR-SEP).
  2. On iOS (or the web), open the same work order › Details › IR Photo Type › pick FOTRIC-SEP.
  3. On Android, tap Refresh (top right) and wait for "Refreshing" to finish. Repeat once.
  4. Go back to the Work Orders list and open the work order again.
Expected

After Refresh, Android shows FOTRIC-SEP and the FOTRIC Camera card, as iOS does after one Refresh.

Actual

Android still shows FLIR-SEP and the FLIR Camera card after two Refreshes. It changes only after the work order is opened again (sometimes only after the Work Orders list is refreshed, or the app is reopened).

Why it matters: the type decides the capture flow (FLIR or FOTRIC camera, SEP or IND photos). A technician on Android can keep capturing with the wrong camera flow after the office changes the type.
